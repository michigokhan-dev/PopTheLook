This Cookie Policy explains how POP THE LOOK may use cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website. Cookies are small text files that a website can store on your device. Similar technologies may store or access information on your device or help the website remember activity.

1. Types of cookies

The final website may use the following categories, depending on the features that are enabled:

  • Strictly necessary cookies: help core website features work, such as security, navigation, and remembering essential choices.
  • Preference cookies: remember choices you make, where this feature is available.
  • Analytics cookies: help us understand how visitors use the website so we can improve its performance, if analytics tools are enabled and the required consent has been obtained.
  • Marketing cookies: may be used to measure campaigns or personalise advertising, if these tools are introduced and you have provided any consent required by law.

These are possible categories, not a claim that every category is currently active. We will publish an accurate list of the technologies used by the live website before launch.

2. How we use cookies

Depending on the features in use, cookies and similar technologies may help us keep the website secure, support account or cart functions, remember preferences, measure website performance, and understand the effectiveness of marketing. We will not use optional cookies that require consent until the required consent has been given.

3. Your choices and consent

Where required by law, optional cookies will only be set after you have given consent. You should be able to accept or reject optional cookies and change your choice later through the website's cookie settings. Strictly necessary technologies may operate without consent where the law permits this.

Before launch: implement and test a cookie banner or settings control if required. Do not publish controls that look functional but do not actually save or apply the visitor's choice.

4. Managing cookies in your browser

Most web browsers allow you to view, block, or delete cookies through their settings. Blocking certain cookies may affect website features, including sign-in or cart functionality. Browser controls do not necessarily replace consent choices that must be offered by the website.

5. Third-party technologies

Some features may rely on third-party providers, for example hosting, security, analytics, embedded content, or payment processing.
