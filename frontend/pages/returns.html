We want you to feel confident shopping with POP THE LOOK. This policy explains how to contact us about cancelling an order, returning an item, or requesting a refund. Your legal rights as a consumer are not affected by this policy.

1. Contact us before returning an item

Please email popthelookmessages@gmail.com before sending a return. Include your order number, the item you wish to return, and the reason for contacting us. We will explain the next steps and provide the confirmed return instructions.

2. Online order cancellation rights

For many online purchases in the UK, consumers generally have a legal right to cancel within 14 days after receiving goods, followed by a further period to send the goods back. Exceptions can apply to certain products or circumstances. The exact rules depend on applicable law and the type of purchase, so please contact us promptly if you want to cancel an order.

This summary is not a substitute for the legal rights that apply to your purchase. We will provide the cancellation instructions and required information before launch.

3. Return condition

Where you have a right to return an item, please handle it only as much as reasonably necessary to inspect its nature, characteristics, and fit. If an item has been used or handled beyond what is necessary, applicable law may allow a deduction for diminished value. Please include the original packaging and any tags or accessories where reasonably possible, but these requests do not override your statutory rights.

4. Items that may have special return rules

Some products may be subject to legal exceptions to cancellation rights, depending on their nature and condition. Any applicable exception will be clearly explained before purchase. We will not apply a blanket “no returns” rule where it would conflict with consumer law.

5. Faulty, damaged, or incorrect items

If an item arrives faulty, damaged, or different from what you ordered, contact us at popthelookmessages@gmail.com. Include your order number and, if possible, photographs showing the issue. We will review the problem and explain the available remedy under applicable law, which may include a repair, replacement, refund, or another appropriate solution.

Your legal rights for faulty or misdescribed goods are separate from any voluntary returns policy and remain unaffected.
