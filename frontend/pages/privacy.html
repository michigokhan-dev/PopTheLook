Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This is a pre-launch draft. POP THE LOOK's registered business name, business address, final data-retention periods and any additional service providers must be confirmed and this policy reviewed before the store launches.

This Privacy Policy explains how POP THE LOOK (“we”, “us” or “our”) may collect, use, store and share personal information when you visit our website, create an account, contact us or use our services.

1. Who is responsible for your information?

Before launch, this section will be updated with the full legal name and contact address of the business responsible for your personal information. For privacy questions, you can contact us at popthelookmessages@gmail.com.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on the features you use, we may collect:

  • Account information: your name and email address, plus the password-related information handled by our authentication provider.
  • Profile and delivery information: phone number, delivery address, town or city, postcode and country, if you choose to provide them.
  • Shopping information: products you view or add to your basket and, once ordering is enabled, order details and purchase history.
  • Messages: information you provide when contacting customer support.
  • Newsletter preferences: your email address or phone number and your consent or opt-in status, if you subscribe when these features are enabled.
  • Technical information: information necessary to operate, protect and troubleshoot the website, which may be processed by our hosting and service providers.

3. How we use information

We use personal information only where there is an appropriate lawful basis, such as taking steps at your request, performing a contract, meeting legal obligations, pursuing legitimate interests where appropriate, or relying on your consent. Purposes may include:

  • creating and managing customer accounts;
  • saving profile details and supporting shopping-basket features;
  • responding to customer enquiries;
  • processing orders and deliveries once checkout is enabled;
  • sending product news or marketing only where the required permission has been obtained;
  • maintaining website security and preventing misuse; and
  • meeting accounting, tax and other legal requirements where applicable.

4. Service providers and sharing

We may share information with service providers only when needed to operate the website or provide a requested service. The website currently uses or is being developed with services including Supabase for database and account-related functionality and GitHub Pages for website hosting. Google Fonts may be used to display website typography.

Payment, email-delivery, analytics or other providers will be identified here if and when they are enabled. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

5. International transfers

Some service providers may process information outside the United Kingdom. Before launch, we will confirm where relevant providers process data and describe the safeguards that apply to any restricted international transfers.

6. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only for as long as needed for the purpose it was collected, including applicable legal, accounting and reporting requirements. Specific retention periods for account, order, support and marketing records will be confirmed before launch. Where information is no longer needed, we will securely delete it or anonymise it where appropriate.

7. Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law may give you the right to request access to your personal information, correct inaccurate information, request erasure or restriction, object to certain processing, receive certain information in a portable format, or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To make a request, email popthelookmessages@gmail.com.

8. Marketing choices

If we introduce email or WhatsApp marketing, we will use the relevant contact details only in accordance with the permissions and rules that apply. You can opt out of marketing at any time using the unsubscribe method provided or by contacting us. Essential service messages, such as account or order updates, are different from marketing messages.

9. Cookies and similar technologies

The website may use essential browser storage or similar technologies to support features such as the shopping basket, account sessions and remembering a website preference. Any non-essential cookies or tracking technologies will be assessed and, where required, used only after valid consent. More detailed information will be provided in our Cookie Policy before launch.

10. Keeping information secure

We take reasonable technical and organisational steps to protect personal information. No method of online transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Children's privacy

This website is intended for people who can legally make purchases. We do not knowingly seek to collect children's personal information unnecessarily. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us so we can review the situation.

12. Complaints

Please contact us first if you have a concern about how we handle your information. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website develops or our legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

14. Contact us

For privacy questions, email popthelookmessages@gmail.com. The business's full legal name and postal address will be added here before launch.
